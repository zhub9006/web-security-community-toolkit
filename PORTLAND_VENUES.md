## 🚌 Transit & Accessibility Notes

- **MAX Light Rail:** Pioneer Courthouse Square, PCC Downtown, OCC, and most downtown venues are on the MAX Blue, Red, Green, and Orange lines.
- **Portland Streetcar:** Connects downtown to PSU and the Pearl District.
- **Bike:** Portland is a Bike City; marked bike lanes abound; most venues within 2 km are bike-accessible.
- **Parking:** Street parking is metered weekdays; the Smart Park garage network covers downtown.
- **ADA Access:** Verify in advance — some historic buildings have limited elevator access. Oregon Convention Center and PSU are fully ADA-compliant.
